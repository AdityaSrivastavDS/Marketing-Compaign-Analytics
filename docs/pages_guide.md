# Report Pages & Visual Navigation Guide

The dashboard is structured into 7 dedicated analytical pages designed for executive decision-makers, marketing strategists, and category merchandisers.

---

### Page 1: Executive Overview (`ReportSection_ExecOverview`)
- **Primary Goal:** High-level executive pulse check on overall revenue, customer volume, and conversion health.
- **Key Visuals (21 Containers):**
  - KPI Cards: Total Spend, Total Customers, Last Campaign Response %, Total Orders.
  - Revenue by Category: Donut chart displaying product share (Wine leading at 50.2%).
  - Channel Breakdown: Clustered column chart comparing Store, Web, and Catalog orders.
  - Geographic Heatmap: Global order distribution across key regions.
  - Slicers: Year, Country, Education, Marital Status.

---

### Page 2: Demographics & Segmentation (`ReportSection_Demographics`)
- **Primary Goal:** Uncover demographic attributes driving customer value and loyalty.
- **Key Visuals (17 Containers):**
  - Income Distribution vs. Spend: Scatter chart displaying income correlation with spend.
  - Age Group Cohorts: Under 35, 35-49, 50-64, 65+ performance bars.
  - Marital Status Breakdown: Comparative spend across family structures.
  - Household Composition: Impact of kids and teens on product preferences.

---

### Page 3: Product Intelligence (`ReportSection_ProductIntel`)
- **Primary Goal:** Category-level performance, basket mix, and margin potential.
- **Key Visuals (18 Containers):**
  - Spend by Product Category: Detailed bar breakdown across all 6 lines.
  - Average Basket Value per Segment: Product preferences by income band.
  - Category Share Trend: Historical purchasing patterns.
  - Cross-Sell Matrix: Correlation between wine and gourmet meat purchases.

---

### Page 4: Campaign Analytics (`ReportSection_Campaigns`)
- **Primary Goal:** Evaluate promotional wave ROI and multi-touch response.
- **Key Visuals (18 Containers):**
  - Campaign 1-5 Performance Comparison: Acceptance rates across waves.
  - Last Campaign Conversion Funnel: Response rate benchmarking (14.91%).
  - Multi-Campaign Responder Segments: Profiling frequent campaign responders.
  - Complaint Analysis: 21 customer complaints mapped to campaign touches.

---

### Page 5: Channel & Engagement (`ReportSection_Channels`)
- **Primary Goal:** Omnichannel journey tracking and physical vs. digital purchase habits.
- **Key Visuals (18 Containers):**
  - Channel Volume Comparison: Store (46.2%), Web (32.6%), Catalog (21.2%).
  - Deal & Discount Sensitivity: Customers heavily driven by promotions.
  - Web Visits vs. E-Commerce Purchases: Conversion efficiency analysis.

---

### Page 6: Customer Value & Lifetime Value (`ReportSection_CustomerValue`)
- **Primary Goal:** Customer lifetime value, recency, and loyalty tiering.
- **Key Visuals (18 Containers):**
  - RFM Segmentation Matrix: Recency vs. Frequency vs. Monetary value tiers.
  - Top 10% Customer Profiles: Attributes of premium tier accounts.
  - Value Migration: Customer tenure vs. average expenditure.

---

### Page 7: Customer Data Explorer (`ReportSection_DataExplorer`)
- **Primary Goal:** Operational drill-down and record-level inspection.
- **Key Visuals (14 Containers):**
  - Detailed Data Table: Tabular records with interactive sorting.
  - Customer Filter Panel: Multi-parameter search by ID, Country, Income, and Channel.
  - Export Utility: Summarized export capabilities for downstream activation.
